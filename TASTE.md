# Taste

Who's going: one person, mid-40s, East London, single, curious. Works in
tech and law, likes art, writing, ideas, records. Goes out to meet people,
not to sit in a dark room.

## Score 8–10 — would actually book
- Big-name or genuinely interesting speakers (How To Academy, Conway Hall,
  ICA, Whitechapel, LRB): history, politics, science, culture, ideas.
- Creative or odd tech: art + code, AI + culture, demos, hardware hacking,
  music tech, generative stuff, small weird meetups with a point of view.
- Gallery openings, private views, Friday/late-night museum lates.
- Making things with other people: life drawing, printmaking, writing
  workshops, supper clubs, book launches.
- Debates and salons where people actually talk to each other afterwards.

## Score 4–6 — fine, wouldn't cross town
- Standard museum talks, tours, panels with no name attached.
- Generic writing groups, "shut up and write", ordinary craft classes.

## Score 0–3 — no
- Generic tech: "AI networking breakfast", founder pitch nights, growth
  hacking, crypto/web3 anything, corporate B2B networking, "business
  drinks", career fairs, dev-tool user groups.
- Anything online/hybrid, kids/family, schools, wellness/coaching/manifesting.
- Ticket-shop noise: "General Admission", venue entry, immersive experiences.
- Jogging, running clubs, run-and-drinks, fitness of any kind.
- Private equity, VC, investing, wealth, HNWI, "capital" anything.
- Black history, equality/diversity/inclusion, activism-as-event.
- Live painting, poetry nights, spoken word, ceramics, pottery.
